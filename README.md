# 🎮 Mibs Console

**Couch games for two players.** One big screen (TV or laptop) + two phones as controllers. No app, no login, free.

**Play here:** https://unfav-mibssm.github.io/console

---

## How to play

**You need:** one TV or laptop screen, two phones, internet.

1. **On the TV / laptop** open the game link and tap **"Show the TV screen"**.
   You will see a 4-letter room code and a QR code.
2. **On each phone** scan the QR code (or open the link, type the code) and enter your nickname.
3. **Turn your phone sideways.** That is your controller.
4. **Player 1** picks a game and taps **Start game**.
5. Play! When it ends, tap **Play again** or go **Back to lobby**.

## The controller

Every game uses the same controller:

- **Left side:** a direction pad. Keep your thumb on it and slide to change direction.
- **Right side:** the big **A** button.
- **Middle:** shows the game name and what the buttons do in that game.

If your phone disconnects for a moment, don't worry. The game waits for you and your seat is kept. Just reopen the page.

## The games

| Game | What to do |
|---|---|
| 🥋 Bumper Sumo | Steer left/right, hold **A** to dash. Push your rival out of the shrinking ring. |
| 🏍️ Light Cycles | Steer with the D-pad. Don't crash into walls or trails. |
| 💪 Tug of War | Tap **A** as fast as you can. |
| 🏓 Pong | Move your paddle up/down. First to 5 wins. |
| ☄️ Meteor Dodge | Move left/right to dodge falling rocks. 3 hits and you're out. |
| 🏒 Air Hockey | Slide your mallet, hold **A** to boost. First to 5 goals. |
| 🎯 Tank Duel | Drive with the D-pad, fire with **A**. Shots bounce once. First to 3 rounds. |
| 💣 Bomber Arena | Walk, drop bombs with **A**, blast blocks and your rival. Best of 3. |

## Tips

- Use fullscreen (⛶ button) on the phone and TV for the best feel.
- Same Wi-Fi is not needed, but a good connection means less lag.
- On a laptop you can test with keys: arrows/WASD to move, Space to press A.
- If something looks stuck, refresh the page.
